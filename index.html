Official Photo & Signature Guidelines for Bangladesh Online Forms

BCS & Teletalk Govt Jobs (300×300 & 300×80)

For all Bangladesh Civil Service (BCS), Teletalk (alljobs.teletalk.com.bd), Primary Teacher Recruitment, NTRCA, and BPSC online job applications, the mandatory specifications are:

  • Applicant Photo: Exactly 300 × 300 pixels (Width 300px, Height 300px), JPG format, file size strictly under 100 KB (maximum 99 KB).
  • Applicant Signature: Exactly 300 × 80 pixels (Width 300px, Height 80px), JPG format, file size strictly under 60 KB.

DUET Admission Form (250×250 & 250×50)

For Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET) undergraduate and postgraduate online admission forms:

  • Applicant Photo: Exactly 250 × 250 pixels, JPG format, file size strictly under 100 KB.
  • Applicant Signature: Exactly 250 × 50 pixels, JPG format, file size strictly under 50 KB.
