Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DUET admission photo and signature size?

DUET requires Photo: 250×250 px (≤ 100 KB), Signature: 250×50 px (≤ 50 KB) in JPG format.

How does DUET differ from standard government job portals?

Government job portals use 300×300 and 300×80, while DUET uses a specialized 250×250 and 250×50 standard.
